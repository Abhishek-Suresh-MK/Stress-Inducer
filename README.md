# Stress-Inducer
Stress Inducer is a quirky and intentionally frustrating project built during the TinkerHub USELESS Project Overnight Hackathon. The goal of the hackathon was to create something useless yet interesting — and that’s exactly what we did.

While most people play games to relieve stress, we flipped the concept on its head and asked: What if someone actually wants to feel more stressed? Thus, Stress Inducer was born — a chaotic website designed to mess with your brain.

The site features four mini-games, each carefully crafted to push your patience to its limits and spike your stress levels in the most annoying ways possible.
